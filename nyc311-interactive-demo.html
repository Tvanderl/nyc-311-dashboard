How this maps to Power BI

The filters are Power BI slicers. The rankings act as cross-filtering visuals. The selection drill-down is equivalent to a drill-through page.

Budget: actual request volume for the matching prior-year month plus 5%.

Forecast: a 3-month moving average with rolling MSE.

The source does not consistently expose a usable formal due date for every case, so this demo does not claim SLA compliance.

Methods & quality

This page makes the dashboard logic transparent for the management team and supports the assignment’s situation-awareness and maintainability requirements.

SA level 1 · Perception

What is happening?

Request volume, peak month, top agencies, problem types and borough demand show the current operational environment.

SA level 2 · Comprehension

Why does it matter?

Cross-filters, actual-versus-budget variance, ranked operational owners and the drill-through page explain where demand differs from plan.

SA level 3 · Projection

What is likely next?

The three-month moving average, forecast MSE and risk/opportunity tornado provide a transparent forward-looking planning signal.

Data and refresh

Live-source governance

Source: NYC Open Data — 311 Service Requests from 2020 to Present (dataset erm2-nwe9).

Refresh: visuals retrieve official Socrata aggregates and drill-through records when the selection changes. A Power BI version should schedule refresh after the dataset’s daily update.

Data preparation: standardise date fields, retain request ID as the fact-table key, map agency/borough/problem dimensions and calculate the planning and forecast measures in the semantic model.

KPI definitions

Management measures

KPIDefinition
Request volumeCount of NYC 311 requests created in the selected period.
Budget varianceActual requests less matching prior-year monthly requests × 1.05.
ForecastAverage of the three immediately preceding monthly actual values.
MSEMean of squared rolling moving-average forecast errors.
